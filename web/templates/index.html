BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Know your gestures.

Five simple controls keep your hands in the flow.

☝
RULE 01

Index finger

DRAW

Raise your index finger and keep your thumb apart. Move your hand to draw in the air.

🤏
RULE 02

Pinch to pause

PAUSE

Bring your index finger and thumb close together to pause your stroke.

✊
RULE 03

Closed fist

ERASER

Make a fist to erase unwanted marks. Open your hand to return to drawing.

✋
RULE 04

Open palm

CHANGE COLOR

Show your open palm briefly to move to the next drawing color.

🤘
RULE 05

Sign of the horns

EXIT STUDIO

Hold this sign briefly to close the drawing studio and return home.

✦

Quick setup

A few small things make tracking work better.

  • Allow camera access for Python in your system privacy settings.
  • Keep your hand visible inside the camera frame.
  • Use a well-lit space.
  • Keep a comfortable, steady distance from the camera.
